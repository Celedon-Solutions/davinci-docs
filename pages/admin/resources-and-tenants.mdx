## Overview

The **Projects**, **Organizations**, and **Tenants** pages provide system-level views of Davinci resources. Use them for support, ownership changes, recovery, entitlement and accounting management, and deployment administration rather than ordinary day-to-day collaboration.

For user-facing resource concepts, start with:

- [Projects](/pages/browser/projects)
- [Organizations](/pages/browser/organizations)
- [Teams](/pages/browser/teams)
- [Groups](/pages/browser/groups)
- [Roles And Permissions](/pages/browser/roles-permissions)

## Projects

Use **Administration → Projects** (`/admin/~/projects`) to search across accessible projects, inspect metadata and storage, and perform project-level administration.

### Find A Project

Search by project name or slug. Filters are available for:

- Visibility: private, internal, or public.
- A user UUID as owner or member.
- Last updated within 7, 30, or 90 days.
- Storage above 100 MB, 1 GB, or 10 GB.

The project table shows owner, visibility, effective member count, storage, and creation and update dates.

### Project Details

Select a project to open four tabs:

- **Details** — identifiers, owner, visibility, direct and effective membership, transfer state, storage, and timestamps.
- **Members** — direct and inherited access.
- **Storage** — current project storage.
- **Actions** — rename, transfer ownership, or schedule deletion.

### Manage Collaborators

The Members tab distinguishes:

- **Direct collaborators**, whose role can be changed or whose membership can be removed.
- **Organization members**, whose access is inherited from the owning organization.
- **The owner**, who cannot be managed as an ordinary collaborator.

Add a direct collaborator by user ID and choose Viewer, Project Editor, or Admin. Removing a direct collaborator removes project access immediately.

Inherited organization access cannot be changed from the project. Update the user's organization membership instead.

### Transfer Ownership

Project ownership can be transferred to another user or organization. A user target can be identified by email, username, or UUID; an organization target uses its UUID.

An organization transfer can complete immediately when the required access is available. A user transfer can create a transfer request for the recipient. Review the project's **Transfer State** after submitting the action.

Before transferring:

1. Confirm the destination account or organization.
2. Verify that the destination should own storage and lifecycle responsibility.
3. Review organization visibility and inherited access.
4. Complete any billing or retention checks required by your deployment.

Transfer is different from adding a collaborator: the destination becomes responsible for the project.

### Deleted Projects

Deleting an active project schedules it for permanent deletion after the deployment's grace period. The **Deleted** tab shows:

- Project and owner.
- Deletion date.
- Days remaining.
- Storage usage.
- Restore and Hard Delete actions.

**Restore** returns an eligible project to active state. **Hard Delete** immediately removes the project and wipes its storage.

<Warning>
Hard deletion cannot be undone. Use the displayed days remaining rather than assuming a fixed recovery period; deployments can configure the grace window.
</Warning>

## Organizations

Use **Administration → Organizations** (`/admin/~/orgs`) to create, inspect, edit, or delete organizations and to manage organization entitlements, AI funding, billing, and storage overrides.

### Create An Organization

Select **Add Organization** and provide:

- **Name** and **Handle** (`slug`).
- **Tenant**.
- **Owner** from that tenant (populated after selecting the tenant).
- Optional **Description**.
- Optional **Starting Credit Balance (USD)** when organization AI funding is enabled in System Settings.
- **Private** or **Public** visibility.

Organization visibility affects whether the organization and its public projects can be accessed publicly. See [Organization Visibility](/pages/browser/organizations#organization-visibility).

### Organization Details

Selecting an organization row opens a six-tab detail modal:

- **Info** — avatar (with option to remove custom avatar image), identifiers, handle/slug, tenant, visibility, description, and creation date.
- **Members** — current organization members and their roles.
- **Entitlements** — workspace entitlement group memberships and per-organization overrides for workspace keys (projects owned by the organization resolve workspace entitlements from the organization rather than the acting user). See [Entitlement Groups](/pages/admin/user-access#entitlement-groups-administration--groups).
- **Billing** — the organization's accounting pools (**Included Usage**, **Credit Balance**, and **On-Demand Usage**), AI funding controls, storage grace deadline, and member spending limits.
- **Storage** — current storage usage, effective limit, and manual storage limit override in GB.
- **Edit** — update name, slug, visibility, and description, or delete the organization.

### Organization Billing, AI Funding, And Storage Grace

When the organization's entitlements allow it to pay for AI usage (`Organization AI Funding` in System Settings or an organization entitlement group/override), the **Billing** tab exposes:

- **Adjust Credit Balance** — **Add** or **Deduct** USD on the organization's Credit Balance with a required reason.
- **Organization Pays for AI Usage** — toggle whether AI usage in the organization's projects is billed to the organization (`org`) or to individual members (`members`).
- **On-Demand Usage Limit** — set the organization's per-period on-demand limit (`Off`, `$25`, `$50`, `$100`, `$500`, `Unlimited`, or custom amount) and per-member spending limits.
- **Storage Grace Deadline** — set or clear the date by which the organization's storage usage must return under its limit or be covered by a storage tier.

### Storage Override

On the **Storage** tab, a manual organization storage limit override (in GB) supersedes the automatic plan or tier limit. Selecting **Reset to Billing Plan** clears the manual override.

The override updates Davinci's organization accounting record; it does not modify external payment-provider subscription items.

### Delete An Organization

Organization deletion is presented as an irreversible action on the **Edit** tab.

<Warning>
Resolve or transfer projects, storage, subscription state, and ownership responsibilities before deleting an organization, then verify those resources afterward.
</Warning>

## Tenants

The **Tenants** page (`/admin/~/tenants`) is available on multi-tenant deployments. It lets administrators search and filter tenants by **Billing Mode** (`Team` or `Individual`) and **Subscription Status** (`Active`, `Trial`, or `No subscription`), and displays each tenant's user count, organization count, and creation date.

### Create A Tenant

Select **Add Tenant**, enter a **Name**, and choose:

- **Team (shared AI pool, seats, & organizations)** for a group-managed tenant.
- **Individual** for independently managed accounts.

You can optionally select or enter an **Initial Owner / Admin** (who is moved into the tenant as `tenant_admin` with an initial organization created automatically). When trial creation is enabled for a Team tenant, check **Create as trial group** to configure:

- **Trial Duration (days)** within the deployment's maximum limit.
- **Included Usage per Month (USD)**.
- **Seat Limit** and **Organization Limit**.
- **Reason** (internal note).

### Tenant Detail Tabs

Selecting a tenant opens the Tenant Details modal, which provides a **Switch Admin Context to Tenant** button in the footer and five tabs:

#### 1. Overview & Settings
- Edit the tenant's **Name**, **Billing Mode** (`Team` or `Individual`), and **Tenant Owner**.
- **Trial Management** (when the tenant is on an active trial): Adjust the **Trial End Date**, **Included Usage per Month (USD)**, **Seat Limit**, **Organization Limit**, and **Internal Reason**, or click **Clear Trial Now** to end the trial immediately.
- **Disband & Delete Tenant** (for non-default tenants): Detaches all members back to the `default` tenant, detaches all organizations to standalone ownership, closes the tenant accounting record, and deletes the tenant.

#### 2. Members
- **Move / Add Existing User to This Tenant**: Pick a standalone user or enter an email, username, or UUID, choose their **Tenant Role** (`Member`, `Tenant Admin`, `Tenant Billing`, or `Tenant Support`) and **Seat Type** (`Full Seat` or `Read-only Seat`), and optionally check **Also attach standalone organizations owned by this user into the tenant**.
- **Current Members Table**: Change any member's **Tenant Role** or **Seat Type** (`Full` / `Read-only`) inline, or click **Detach to Default** to move the user back to the `default` tenant.

#### 3. Organizations
- **Attach Existing Standalone Organization**: Select a standalone organization to link into the tenant.
- **Tenant Organizations Table**: View all organizations in the tenant, jump to their detail view on the Organizations page, or click **Detach to Standalone**.

#### 4. Subscription
- Inspect or configure the tenant's **Edition** (`Hosted / Team (everything)` or `Community`), **Full seats** cap, **Read-only seats** cap, **Organizations** cap, **Ends** date, and **Trial** flag/reason. Subscriptions managed by an external billing provider are displayed read-only.

#### 5. Accounting
- View the tenant's accounting pools (**Included Usage**, **Credit Balance**, and **On-Demand Usage**), adjust balances in USD, configure the tenant's on-demand limit and shared included-usage settings, and manage default or per-member spending limits.
