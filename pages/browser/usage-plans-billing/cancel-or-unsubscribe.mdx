## Overview

Cancellation behavior depends on whether you are managing an individual plan, an organization, a group, or an enterprise-managed deployment.

Before canceling, export important data, review project ownership, and make sure you understand what happens to usage, storage, organizations, and group membership.

## Individual Plans

To cancel an individual plan when self-service billing is available:

1. Open the Davinci Browser.
2. Click your avatar.
3. Open **Settings**.
4. Go to **Plan & Invoices**.
5. Under **Subscription**, click **Cancel at Period End** next to the renewal date. You can also click **Manage** next to **Billing Portal** to use the Stripe customer portal.

Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. Until then, you can click **Resume** to keep it. Any unbilled on-demand usage is invoiced when the subscription ends.

If your account is managed by an enterprise deployment, contact your administrator instead.

## Leaving A Group

If you are a group member but not the group owner, you may be able to leave the group from **Settings → Plan & Invoices** (**Leave Group**). Leaving a group removes access to group billing. You return to your previous personal plan if it has not run out yet; otherwise, your account reverts to the Free plan. Your Credit Balance is preserved.

## Disbanding A Group

Group owners and billing administrators may be able to disband a group from **Group → Settings** (**Disband Group**) or from **Settings → Plan & Invoices**. Disbanding a group:

- Permanently dissolves the group.
- Cancels the group subscription with proration.
- Returns members to their personal plan if it is still active, or to the Free plan.
- Deletes the group's organizations and moves their active projects to each organization owner's account.

Disbanding is a high-impact action. Confirm project ownership and exports before proceeding.

## Organizations

Organization billing and storage may be direct, group-managed, or enterprise-managed. Review organization ownership and project ownership before canceling a related plan or storage tier.

Deleting an organization removes all members. Active projects owned by the organization are moved to the organization owner's account. Projects already in the organization's trash are permanently deleted.

## Data Warning

<Warning>
Deleting an account, disbanding a group, deleting an organization, or canceling a plan can affect access to projects and stored data. Export important work before making irreversible changes.
</Warning>

## Need Help?

If you are unsure which cancellation path applies, contact your administrator or Celedon support before making changes.
