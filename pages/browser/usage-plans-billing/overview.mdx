## Overview

Davinci deployments can be self-serve, enterprise-managed, or privately administered. Because of that, billing is best understood as one part of account, organization, and group management rather than the center of every workflow.

For current public pricing and enterprise options, see [Celedon pricing](https://celedon.solutions/pricing).

## Individual Plans

Individual plans apply to user accounts. Paid individual tiers can unlock higher limits, more included usage, on-demand usage options, more storage, and organization creation.

See [Individual Plans](/pages/getting-started/plans) for the public self-serve plan comparison.

To change an individual plan, see [Change An Individual Plan](/pages/browser/usage-plans-billing/change-individual-plan).

## Included Usage And On-Demand Usage

Included usage is the usage provided with a subscription or deployment allocation. On-demand usage, when available, lets eligible users or groups continue using paid AI features after included usage is exhausted.

Not every deployment exposes the same billing controls. Enterprise deployments may manage usage and access centrally.

## How On-Demand Usage Is Billed

On-demand usage is not charged one request at a time. It builds up as an unbilled balance on the account that pays for it: you, your organization, or your group.

- When the unbilled balance reaches $100, Davinci issues an invoice for the full unbilled amount and charges the payment method on file right away.
- Any unbilled amount left at the end of a billing period is invoiced when the subscription renews.
- If a subscription ends, any remaining unbilled usage is invoiced at that time.
- On yearly plans, unbilled usage is still invoiced at least once a month.

On-demand invoices are separate from subscription invoices. They are labeled **Davinci On-Demand AI Usage** with the date range they cover, and they appear in the **Invoices** list on **Plan & Invoices** for the paying account.

If an invoice payment fails, on-demand usage is paused until the past-due invoice is settled. Included usage and Credit Balance remain available in the meantime, and **Wallet & Limits** shows a banner with a link to your invoices.

<Note>
$100 is the default amount. Enterprise and private deployments may be configured with a different amount, or may not bill on-demand usage through self-service invoices at all.
</Note>

## Storage

Storage limits apply to uploaded files and project data. Storage can be associated with a user account, organization, or group depending on who owns the project and how the deployment is configured.

## Organizations

Organizations can own projects, manage members, and carry their own storage and administrative controls. Paid user tiers can unlock organization creation in self-serve deployments.

When enabled for your deployment, an organization can pay for AI usage in its own projects instead of its members paying. Organization admins can set per-member spending limits and, on some deployments, add the organization's own AI provider API keys. See [AI Usage Funding](/pages/browser/organizations#ai-usage-funding).

See [Organizations](/pages/browser/organizations) and [Add An Organization](/pages/browser/usage-plans-billing/add-organization).

## Groups

Groups provide centralized management for users, organizations, projects, usage, billing, and governance. A group can include multiple organizations and manage usage and spending budgets across members.

See [Groups](/pages/browser/groups) and [Upgrade To A Group](/pages/browser/usage-plans-billing/upgrade-to-group).

## Where To Manage Billing

Depending on your permissions and deployment:

- Individual users manage their personal plan and invoices from **Settings → Plan & Invoices**, and their balances and on-demand limit from **Settings → Wallet & Limits**.
- Organization admins manage organization storage, project ownership settings, AI usage funding, and member spending limits from the organization's **Wallet & Limits** and **Plan & Invoices** tabs.
- Group admins and billing roles manage group-level billing, member usage limits, organization storage tiers, and administrative controls.
- Enterprise users may need to contact their system administrator.

## Canceling Or Unsubscribing

Cancellation behavior depends on whether you are managing an individual plan, group, organization, or enterprise deployment. See [Cancel Or Unsubscribe](/pages/browser/usage-plans-billing/cancel-or-unsubscribe).
