## Version 5.2.5 - Organization AI Funding & Matrix Sorting

This patch lets organizations pay for their members' AI usage, adds sorting to matrices, and improves slide export.

### Organization AI Funding

- [Organizations can now pay for their members' AI (LLM) usage](/pages/browser/organizations#ai-usage-funding), with organization API keys for model providers and default and per-member spending limits
- Usage and charges now show who paid for each AI request
- Updated the [usage and billing](/pages/browser/usage-plans-billing/overview) pages

### Matrices, Slides & Exports

- [Matrices](/pages/modeling/objects/matrix#row-sorting) can now sort rows by one or more columns
- Improved slide export speed and reliability
- Fixed slide images and videos not being kept as project references
